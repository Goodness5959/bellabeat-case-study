# 📊 Bellabeat Smart Device Case Study: Activity & Sleep Patterns

## Executive Summary
This case study analyzes smart device usage data from female consumers to identify activity patterns and sleep tracking behaviors. The primary goal is to provide evidence-based marketing strategies and product recommendations to support **Bellabeat’s** growth in the global smart device market.

## 📈 Key Analytical Insights

1. **Activity vs. Sleep Correlation:**
   * Correlation analysis reveals **no strong direct relationship** between daytime activity levels (active minutes or daily step counts) and nightly sleep duration. High physical exertion during the day does not automatically yield longer or better sleep.

2. **The Overnight Compliance Gap:**
   * There is a **significant drop-off in overnight sleep tracking compliance** compared to daytime step logging. Users regularly remove their smart devices before bed, likely due to battery charging routines or overnight wearability preferences.

---

## 💡 Strategic Marketing & Product Recommendations

1. **Promote Strategic Daytime Battery Charging:**
   * Introduce automated smart notifications prompting users to charge their device during evening unwind time (*"Charge while you unwind, wear while you sleep"*), ensuring devices remain on overnight.

2. **Pivot Marketing Messaging:**
   * Shift promotional messaging away from framing sleep purely as a reward for fitness. Market Bellabeat devices around overall sleep hygiene, nightly wind-down routines, and 24/7 holistic wellness tracking.

3. **Highlight 24/7 Comfort & Wearability:**
   * Design marketing campaigns emphasizing lightweight, ergonomic designs and long battery life to build consumer confidence in wearing devices seamlessly through the night.
