# 📊 Bellabeat Smart Device Case Study: Activity & Sleep Patterns

## Executive Summary
This case study analyzes smart device usage data to understand daily activity habits and sleep tracking behavior. The goal is to deliver practical, data-backed marketing strategies to help **Bellabeat** grow its brand and better connect with users.

🔗 **[View Interactive Tableau Dashboard](https://public.tableau.com/authoring/Bellabeatsmartdevicecasestudy/Dashboard1/Logged%20Daily%20Activity%20Vs%20Total%20Sleep#1)**

---

## 📈 Key Analytical Insights

1. **Activity Doesn't Drive Sleep Duration:**
   * Daytime workouts and step counts show **no strong correlation** with nightly sleep. High physical activity during the day doesn't automatically mean a user sleeps longer or better.

2. **The Overnight Sleep-Tracking Drop-Off:**
   * Far fewer users track their sleep compared to their daytime steps. Many users take their devices off at night, likely due to uncomfortable wear or routine battery charging.

---

## 💡 Practical Marketing & Product Recommendations

1. **Fix the Charging Habit:**
   * Right now, people take their trackers off at night to charge them. Use gentle app reminders to encourage evening charging (*"Charge while you unwind, wear while you sleep"*) so devices stay on overnight.

2. **Change How We Talk About Sleep:**
   * Stop treating sleep like a bonus prize you only get if you hit your workout goals. Market sleep tracking on its own terms as a core part of nightly relaxation and general wellness, even on rest days.

3. **Highlight 24/7 Comfort:**
   * Focus marketing campaigns on how light, stylish, and comfortable the device is to wear continuously—day and night.
